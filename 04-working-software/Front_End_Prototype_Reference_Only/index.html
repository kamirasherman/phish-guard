How to identify phishing indicators

Short training content that can be triggered after a click or landing-page visit.

2 minutes
🔗

Check the link

Hover over links and confirm the destination matches the organization before clicking.

⏱️

Question urgency

Be cautious of messages that pressure you to act immediately or threaten account loss.

✉️

Verify sender details

Review sender addresses carefully, especially small spelling changes or unusual domains.

🔐

Never enter passwords

Use trusted bookmarks or official portals instead of links from unexpected messages.
