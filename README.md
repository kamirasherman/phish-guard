# Phish-Guard | Project Manager: Kamira Sherman

Phishing simulation & awareness-training platform — M.S. in Information Technology
capstone, Capella University (September 2026).

## My Role

I was the **Project Manager**, accountable for planning, governance, and delivery from
charter through final handoff. I led a four-person Agile team across three sprints —
managing scope, schedule, risk, and stakeholder expectations for a security-sensitive
build with strict ethical constraints (simulated data only: no real PII, no credential
collection).

## What I Led

- **Charter & scope** — defined objectives, scope boundaries, constraints, and success
  criteria; held the line on scope as requirements evolved mid-project.
- **Agile delivery** — ran sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives across 3 sprints;
  maintained the product backlog, Jira board, and sprint review packets for stakeholder
  sign-off.
- **Governance & traceability** — decision log, workload matrix, dependency matrix, and a
  requirements traceability matrix linking every backlog item to its acceptance criteria,
  validation method, and ethical/security control.
- **Risk management** — tracked risks, constraints, and assumptions with documented
  mitigation strategies, and adapted plans when dependencies threatened delivery.
- **Stakeholder communication** — biweekly client recaps, weekly status reports, and
  secured written client authorization before any simulated-user pilot testing began.
- **Team leadership** — replanned workload and responsibilities through a mid-project
  team transition so milestones held without slipping.
- **Transition & handoff** — authored the transition strategy and implementation roadmap
  so a future team can continue the work with full context.

## Outcome

Delivered a working phishing simulation platform (campaign management, event tracking,
training quiz, reporting dashboard, role-based access control), a completed
simulated-user pilot with documented client authorization, and a complete handoff
package — on a fixed academic timeline.
